Reading Comprehension: Academic Passage

The modern research landscape has undergone an unprecedented epistemic transition. Traditionally anchored in autonomous philosophical wonder and the pursuit of objective truth, contemporary universities are increasingly governed by market-driven metrics, quantifiable output indexes, and commodified patent quotas. While institutional accountability ensures fiscal transparency, it has inadvertently fostered a hyper-competitive 'publish-or-perish' ecosystem. In this system, exploratory long-term inquiries are routinely marginalized in favor of incremental, low-risk investigations. Genuine scientific progress demands not merely bureaucratic surveillance, but an institutional culture grounded in intellectual courage, methodological skepticism, and ethical responsibility toward ecological and social well-being.
